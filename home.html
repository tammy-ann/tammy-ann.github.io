Pilates Reformer

As a Stott certified Reformer instructor, I cater to everyone from beginners to those seeking a more challenging workout. With Level 1 and Level 2 Reformer training, I adapt each session to meet individual needs and goals. 

My passion lies in Pilates, especially Reformer. I love helping clients grow and reach their goals, incorporating mindfulness along the way. As a mother of two, I understand the challenge of finding time for personal growth. During my two years in Singapore, I was fortunate to join a wonderful team and earn my STOTT Reformer qualification, which deepened my skills and awareness. My love for Reformer truly blossomed when I sustained an injury; it allowed me to maintain my fitness and recover, showcasing its versatility in both rehab and strength building. With or without injury, Reformer ensures continuous progress and strength.
