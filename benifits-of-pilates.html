Benefits

Benefits of Pilates and Reformer

STOTT Pilates highlights several benefits, including improved core strength, better posture and alignment, enhanced flexibility and mobility, and greater body awareness. It’s adaptable for all ages and fitness levels, making it suitable for both rehabilitation and athletic training. It also focuses on restoring spinal curves and balancing muscle groups around joints. Overall, it’s a comprehensive approach that blends strength, flexibility, and mindfulness. The other Benefits also include being present and looking after you.

Benefits of Personal Training

The benefits of personal training include becoming your best self, challenging yourself to grow mind, body, and soul, exploring new activities, meeting new people, and connecting with groups that align with your fitness goals. It also nurtures both your brain and body, helps you stay injury-free, and provides a purposeful path toward fitness and longevity, positively impacting your state of mind each day.

Its also great to have a trainer you connect with who understands your strength and weaknesses and listens to help you grow.
